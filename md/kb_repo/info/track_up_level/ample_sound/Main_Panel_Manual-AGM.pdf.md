2.2 Articulations & Poly Legato



2.2.1 Articulation List


Abbr.	Full Name	Keyswitch	Range

Sus	Sustain & Pop	C0	E1-C5

NH	Natural Harmonic	C#0	E2-C5

PM	Palm Mute	D0	E1-C5

SIO	Slide In & Slide Out	D#0	F#1-C5

LS	Legato Slide (Poly Legato)	E0	F1-C5

HP	Hammer-On & Pull-Off	F0	E1-C5

SG	Slide Guitar	F#0	F#1-C5




2.2.2 Sustain & Pop


Keyswitch is C0.

l Notes with velocity <127 trigger a sustain note.


l Notes with velocity 127 trigger a Pop.


2.2.3 Natural Harmonic


Keyswitch is C#0.


2.2.4 Palm Mute


Keyswitch is D0.

Lower the velocity, greater the mute depth.


2.2.5 Slide In & Slide Out


Keyswitch is D#0.

l Press D#0 then play a note (above fret 2) will trigger Slide In.

l Press D#0 while a note (above fret 3) is playing will trigger Slide Out.

l Reverts to Sustain automatically.



2.2.6 Legato Slide (Poly Legato)


Keyswitch is E0.

l Press E0, then play two overlapping notes, and the slide between the notes will trigger.


l Support controllable slide speed (by note velocity) and intervals.

l Reverts to previous articulation automatically after slide.

l E0 with high velocity will change position. Otherwise, the position will not be changed by the slide.


Poly Legato: Play multiple start notes on different strings at the same time, then play a higher or lower destination note. All start notes on different strings will trigger slides of the same interval, performing polyphonic legato.

Slide Smoother: For slides over two frets, the destination note's velocity determines the slide speed. Higher the velocity, faster the speed.

2.2.7 Hammer-On & Pull-Off (Poly Legato)


Keyswitch is F0.

l Press F0, then two overlapping notes will trigger HP between the notes.

l Reverts to previous articulation automatically after HP.

l F0 with high velocity will change position, otherwise the position will not be changed by HP.



Poly Legato: Play multiple start notes on different strings at the same time, then play a higher or lower destination note. All start notes on different strings will trigger the HP of the same interval, performing polyphonic legato.

2.2.8 Slide Guitar


Keyswitch is F#0.

High velocity triggers a slide ornament.

Low velocity triggers a sustained slide note.


2.2.9 Doubled Keyswitches


Pressing two articulation keyswitches simultaneously will combine both articulations in performance.

C0 and D0 pressed together: Notes of high velocity will be sustained, and notes of low velocity will be palm mute.


* If you have questions about articulations or legato techniques, it is recommended to use

Riff for MIDI creation and editing. Once finished, simply drag Riffer directly into the host’s MIDI track.

Two guitars (left and right) can trigger different samples. The knob adjusts the stereo width between the two guitars.

Resonance is an indispensable part of the expressiveness and realism of an acoustic instrument. Ample Sound Sample Engine can control the Resonance Time and Resonance Volume.

2.6 Rich Fingering Sound


Simulates the variety of fret noises that naturally occur during real performance. FA: Noise of fingers touching the string

FR: Noise of fingers releasing from the string.


2.7 Capo


It simulates the real bass capo. It can transpose the pitch without changing the fingering.


2.8 Position Mode


Two position modes are available: 1.   

Melody Mode: Covers three octaves, suitable for melodic playing.

Keyswitch: B6 low velocity.

2.

Chord Mode: Each position spans five frets, suitable for chord playing.

Keyswitch: B6 high velocity.


2.9 Open String First


E1 A1 D2 G2 B2 and E3 6 notes will be played on open strings.

It can be toggled on with note G#6, High velocity toggles on, Low velocity toggles off.


2.10 Play Mode


Instrument Mode:          Constrained by real instrument rules (e.g., two notes on the same

string cannot sound simultaneously).

Keyboard Mode:  Not limited by real instrument rules.


Solo Mode: Allows only monophonic playing.

Power Chord (Fifth) Mode:  Plays the root and the fifth simultaneously.

Octave Mode:  Plays the root and the octave simultaneously.

* Keyboard Mode and Solo Mode will both disable automatic legato.


2.11 Auto Legato Mode


There are 3 modes: Automatic Slide; Automatic Hammer On and Pull Off; Off.

*High velocity D#6 turns on Automatic Slide mode; Low velocity D#6 turns on Automatic Hammer-On & Pull-Off mode.

2.12 Stroke Noise


A strummed noise will be generated automatically for polyphonic notes.


2.13 Vibrato Wheel


The default is Auto Vibrato Mode.

To change parameters such as vibrato depth or speed, adjust them in Settings.

When the vibrato depth in Settings is set to  ≥  0.75, a vibrato noise will be triggered.


2.14 String Assignment and Position Assignment



2.14.1    String Assignment


Specify a string to be played. G0-C1 correspond to 6th string-1st string. High velocity string force will affect fret position, while low velocity will not.


2.14.2    Position Assignment


Switch to a fret position manually. A yellow line will be displayed after pressing C#1, notifying the notes that represent positions from 0 and up; then press the position note (E1-G#2) to

switch to a particular position.

*A position covers a range of 5 frets (12 frets in Melody mode).
